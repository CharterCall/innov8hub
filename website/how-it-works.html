What Happens After You Book

Automation can feel complicated. Our process is designed to make it simple, practical and low-risk. We help you understand what is worth automating, build the right solution, test it properly, and support you after launch.

The process, step by step

Seven practical steps from your first call to an automation running in your business — with ongoing support from there.

1
First Call

Book your free automation plan

Your first call is a focused conversation about your business. We cover how you currently handle enquiries, bookings, follow-ups, job tracking, invoicing and admin. We want to understand the real day-to-day picture — not just a high-level overview. The more honest you are about what slows you down, the more useful the call will be.

2
Workflow Review

We map your current workflow

Innov8Hub reviews how the business currently handles enquiries, follow-ups, bookings, quotes, job tracking, customer updates and admin tasks. This gives us a clear picture of what is working and where things tend to slip before we suggest anything.

3
Strategy

We identify the best automation opportunities

Not everything should be automated. Innov8Hub recommends the highest-value areas where automation can save time, improve follow-up, reduce missed opportunities, or create better visibility. Some tasks are better left as a human touchpoint. We will tell you which is which.

4
Solution Design

We design the right solution

Based on the workflow review, we design a solution that fits your actual business. Depending on what is needed, this may include:

  • Job tracking and scheduling workflows
  • Automated SMS or email follow-ups
  • Lead capture forms and CRM updates
  • Quoting workflows and booking reminders
  • Payment chasing and overdue invoice workflows
  • Reporting dashboards and AI-assisted admin workflows
5
Build & Test

We build, test and refine

Innov8Hub builds and tests the automation before you see it. We run through key scenarios, check edge cases, and make refinements. Before going live, we walk you through the workflow so you understand what will happen and when. Nothing launches until you are comfortable with how it works.

6
Go Live

You launch with support

Going live is not the end of the process. Innov8Hub monitors the workflow in the early period, resolves any issues that come up, and helps your team use the system confidently. We are available to make small improvements as you start to see how the automation performs in practice.

7
Ongoing

Improve over time

Automation is not a one-off fix. As your business grows, workflows can be improved, expanded or connected to other tools. Regular check-ins allow us to review what is working, identify new opportunities, and make sure the system still fits the way the business operates.

What we need from you

You do not need to know how to build automations. You just need to show us how your business works today.

The more context you can give us, the better the solution we can build. The onboarding process is straightforward and we guide you through each step.

It helps if you can share:

  • The tools and apps you currently use — calendar, CRM, email, spreadsheets, job apps
  • How you currently handle enquiries, bookings, invoices and follow-ups
  • Examples of the messages, forms or spreadsheets you already use
  • Access to relevant tools, where required and agreed
  • Your preferences around how customers should be contacted
  • Feedback during the testing phase before launch
