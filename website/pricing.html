Questions small business owners usually ask

Simple answers about data, SMS, security and what happens if you cancel.

Is my business and customer data secure?

Innov8Hub is designed so each business has its own workspace. Customer, job and invoice information is kept separate between businesses, and access is controlled through secure login. The goal is to keep the system simple for the owner while still treating customer information responsibly.

Who owns the customer and job data?

You do. Your customer, job and invoice data remains your business information. Innov8Hub provides the system, setup and support, but the operating data belongs to the business using it.

Can I export my data if I leave?

Yes. If you cancel, we can help provide a reasonable export of your core business data such as customers, jobs and invoices, subject to your plan, account status and any agreed support arrangements.

How does SMS compliance work?

SMS messages should only be sent to customers or leads where there is a legitimate business reason or appropriate consent. Innov8Hub can help set up practical SMS workflows, but each business is responsible for using SMS in a way that complies with Australian requirements, including consent, identification and opt-out expectations.

Will customers know the message came from my business?

Yes. SMS workflows are written so customers understand which business is contacting them, what the message is about, and what action they need to take.

Can I cancel the monthly subscription?

Yes. The monthly portal and support fee can be cancelled in line with the agreed terms. If you cancel, access to the portal and ongoing support may end after the paid period. Any custom handover or data export support can be discussed as part of cancellation.

Does this replace Xero, MYOB or my accountant?

No. Innov8Hub is designed to help with job tracking, customer follow-up, invoice workflows and operational visibility. It does not replace accounting, tax or bookkeeping advice. Where needed, the system can support cleaner handover to your bookkeeper or accountant.

Do I need to know exactly what automation I need?

No. Most business owners do not need to come with a technical brief. Start by explaining what takes up time, what feels messy and where follow-up breaks down. Innov8Hub will help map the simplest next step.
