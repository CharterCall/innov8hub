Meet Benjamin

Strategy · Operations · Business Growth

Building practical operating systems for small service businesses.

Innov8Hub was built to help service businesses run more smoothly — with practical systems that reduce manual admin, improve follow-up and give owners clearer visibility of their jobs and cash flow.

Benjamin brings a background in strategy, operations, customer experience and process improvement. The goal is not to sell more tech than a business needs — it is to understand how the business actually runs, find where time and money are being lost, and build something practical that helps.

"My approach is simple: start with the real business problem, keep the system easy to use, and only add automation where it genuinely saves time or improves follow-up." — Benjamin

The focus is on helping small service businesses move from reactive, manual ways of working toward clearer, simpler operations — without needing a technical team or complicated software.

Areas of focus: Job tracking and operations design, workflow improvement, automation for enquiries, quotes, invoices and follow-ups, and practical systems built around how service businesses actually work.
